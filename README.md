# GTA 6 Clipper — "GTA 6's jet ski chase is UNREAL"

Entry for make.fun bounty **gta6-clipper-01** — "Clip GTA 6 for the most
YouTube views" ($500, top 20 paid, deadline Mon 23 Nov 2026 08:00 WIB).
Bounty page: https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01

## The clip
15-second vertical Short (1080x1920) cut from Rockstar's GTA VI Trailer 2
(138.0–145.0s): helicopter over the swamp → Jason & Lucia jumping waves on
a jet ski → Jason on a motorcycle with a seaplane overhead → night street.
Hook text "THIS IS A VIDEO GAME?!" in the first seconds, then a make.fun
pitch card: "MAKE CLIPS LIKE THIS WITH AI AND EARN MONEY ON make.fun —
make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01".

Watch the clip: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/PxNnMdwvGKE

## Files
- `main.ts` — Node 22, stdlib only; copies `VIDEO.json` to `/output`.
- `VIDEO.json` — the public YouTube Shorts URL of the clip.
- `clip-final.mp4` is the rendered clip (kept in the work dir, not the repo).
